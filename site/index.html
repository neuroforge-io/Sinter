START WITH THE WORK YOU ALREADY DO

Small tools.
Meaningful time saved.

01 / FUNDING

Find the right opportunity.

Discover grants, retain references, check entered requirements and revisit searches on a schedule.

Source-linked shortlists, not automatic eligibility decisions.
02 / WRITING

Make your case clearly.

Turn context into briefing notes, enquiry letters and agenda items. Keep questions and supporting material together.

Exact-source reports and separately labelled AI drafts.
03 / MEETINGS

Keep a reliable record.

Import transcripts, confirm speakers, preserve corrections and prepare draft minutes. Local speech recognition is an optional source-installation extra.

Original wording and review flags stay available.
04 / SHARED KNOWLEDGE

Put past work to work.

Explore RKC atlases, build source packets and optionally ask Fracture to draft from them. Compile selected files with an installed RKC.

RKC is optional. Canonical evidence is never rewritten by AI.
05 / EVERYDAY ADMIN

Keep everyone aligned.

Prepare volunteer action plans, export confirmed calendar dates and compare document wording without sending anything to an AI.

CSV, calendar and JSON exports you can keep.
06 / YOUR WORKSPACE

Make yourself comfortable.

Choose larger text, a light or dark theme, calmer motion and layout density. Advanced users can choose a compatible API deployment.

A small native launcher. A familiar browser interface.

DESIGNED FOR REAL RESPONSIBILITY

Useful assistance.
Visible boundaries.

Local work stays local.

Reports and preferences are saved on your computer. Search sends your query; AI features send the context you choose to submit. There is no analytics script on this page.

You make the decisions.

Sinter does not submit applications, send official correspondence or approve minutes. Sources can be wrong and AI can make mistakes. Review before relying on a result.

Open, portable, yours.

The application is Apache 2.0. Documents are exportable. The public Fracture service is a preview with availability and capacity limits, not a 24/7 service guarantee.
