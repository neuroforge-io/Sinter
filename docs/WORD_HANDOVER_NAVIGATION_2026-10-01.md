Current development is `0.5.4rc4.dev0`. The published Linux RC3 preview remains
frozen at `246b91e0ee432cb1f6f6fcd17425f55cd7a4cabe`; its installers do not
contain this improvement. No new installed platform is qualified here.

Generated handovers now give a recipient useful internal Word links between
question passage labels, included quotations and their reference keys. Reference
entries retain the exact excerpt ID, source ID, Unicode range and supplied
caption. Missing quotations remain explicitly missing. The optional navigation
admits at most 64 unambiguous generated reference records; larger or altered
records keep all their wording without these links. Navigation does not verify
source facts, eligibility, completeness or authority.

A compact handover no longer asks a recipient to inspect an application Evidence
panel they may not possess. It explains which selected passages are absent and
asks for the full originals or project backup. The explicit selected-evidence
appendix still reproduces every selected passage, not every original document.
Earlier reports, edits, original inputs, citations and source fingerprints remain
unchanged; this is a new derived presentation.

## Separate actual campaign walkthrough

The operator used the in-app browser on an isolated copy of a real NeuroForge
case, using the preceding `e0076d2` runtime. They inspected sources, deliberately
narrowed a question, prepared and edited a handover, saved distinct Word copies,
stopped the service, reopened the saved report, restored a backup as a separate
project and quit through Sinter's interface. All 11 originals and their 200,553
characters, source choices, earlier report and Word bytes were conserved. The
physical user app and user-owned tabs remained untouched. No model request,
spending commitment or grant submission occurred.

The browser tool's initial read truncated a 208,331-character backup field.
Sinter refused that malformed capture. Reading the complete field in chunks
allowed the actual file-chooser restore. This producer limitation is not a
product backup-corruption finding. No browser download was observed within the
bounded five-second wait; the successful text recovery is not download proof.

Real use also exposed remaining deficiencies: broad keyword matching preferred
sender notes over governing rules; explicit source selection still omitted a
selected historical source and cut off a guidelines table. The operator added a
manual reviewer note instead of treating those matches as answers. The report
save label was ambiguous, and confirmed Word paths did not reappear after a cold
report reopen, although the files remained intact. These are recorded for further
improvement. No new whole-product score or 10/10 claim follows from this review.
