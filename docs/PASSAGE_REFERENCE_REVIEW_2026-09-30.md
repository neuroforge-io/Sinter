## Readable references with exact evidence

New source-only handovers use consistent `Passage 1` labels in their questions
and selected notes. A compact final key retains every canonical excerpt ID,
original-source ID, Unicode character range and quoted-above/Evidence-only scope.
The original source arrays, Audit, excerpts, question statuses, retrieval coverage
and fingerprint are unchanged. Legacy reports retain their existing references.

Presentation labels are explicitly registered against the ordered selected
excerpts. Python rejects missing, duplicate, non-string or blank identities,
invalid ranges and wording that differs from the retained original. Browser links
also check unique identities, exact wording and Unicode code-point offsets;
UTF-16 offsets and Unicode normalization cannot silently substitute another span.
Malformed or stale labels stay plain text. Quoted metadata, source wording, code
and links are excluded from label conversion. Document navigation opens the exact
local original; Audit and Evidence do not gain presentation aliases.

The independent critic found a narrow blank-identity gap in direct adapter use,
which was closed without changing legacy mapping behavior. Its broader browser
and Word matrix covers nonzero Unicode ranges, twelve distinct passages,
Evidence-only material, hostile quoted aliases, legacy reports and malformed
registrations. Its receipts distinguish those probes from actual model quality.

## Performance and practical limits

The final source-bound warm local benchmark measured garden preparation at a
median 1.00 ms and a synthetic 300-document, 1,980,000-character workload at
181.04 ms, with 5,283,413 traced peak bytes. Exact excerpts were verified and zero
transport calls occurred. Only three of 1,800 indexed synthetic passages were
selected, representing two documents; this is explicitly not exhaustive review.
`self-audit-artifacts/offline-benchmark-passage-final.json` retains the measurements
and implementation hashes. These are preparation measurements, not browser
responsiveness, comparative speedup or model-quality claims.

The independent practical assessments remain bounded: the compact handover flow
is UX 7, functionality 7.5, aesthetics 7; the campaign card is UX 7,
functionality 7.5, aesthetics 6.5. The latter remains dense. Lexical wording
matches can still be irrelevant and do not answer questions. Human-recorded
responses, follow-up decisions and source-change invalidation need a separate
design. Nothing in this cycle establishes whole-product 10/10, live assistant
qualification, current eligibility or a funding outcome.
